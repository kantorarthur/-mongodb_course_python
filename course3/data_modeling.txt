entities
attributes

relationships: one to one; one to many; many to many

each piece of information in a DB can be related to other information
we call those entities

entities:
-unique
-independent
-represents objects(eg: person, product...)
-represented by documents
-grouped in collections

eg: users entity: _id:... name: ... (the entity is users itself, not the fields and keys)

attributes:
-characteristics that describe an entity
eg: name, description and price of the product
-attributes are represented as field-value pairs

relationships:
one to one - one entity is related to one other entity
eg: a movie is released by a single studio -- therefore the relationship is "one to one"

one to many - one entity is related to multiple other entities
eg: a movie has multiple casts => one to many

many to many - multiple entities are related to multiple other entities
eg: a movie may be shown in many theatres, and the theatres themselfs may show many movies
