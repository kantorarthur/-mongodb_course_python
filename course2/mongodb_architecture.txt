Ways of storing data in MongoDB:
-Documents: Field-value pairs
-Collection: A group of documents that correspond to an entity
-Database: A group of collections it's stored insied a database
-Nodes: A node refers to an individiual MongoDB instance
This instance can be running on a physical machine;virtual machine or container
-Cluster: A group of MongoDB nodes
There are 2 types of clusters: -Replica sets and sharded clusters

-Replica sets: high availability(replication)
